Upload the monthly IPPT/NS FIT monitoring reports. A snapshot is the full set of six files for one report date — the upload is atomic per date (all six land, or none do). Re-uploading a report date replaces its previous ingest. Filenames must be canonical: {REPORT}_{STATE}_{YYYYMMDD}.csv (e.g. IPPT_COMPLETED_20260911.csv); each file may be either CSV or Excel (.xlsx) and the set may mix the two formats.

Upload the six reports

Required files (each as .csv or .xlsx): IPPT_COMPLETED_*, IPPT_FAILED_*, IPPT_NOT_ATTEMPTED_*, NSFIT_COMPLETED_*, NSFIT_IN_PROGRESS_*, NSFIT_NOT_STARTED_* — all with the same YYYYMMDD date. Columns are matched by header name; rows that fail validation are quarantined, not dropped.
